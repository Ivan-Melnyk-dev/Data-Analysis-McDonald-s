# 🍟 McDonald's Sales Dashboard (South America 2022)
*Interactive data analysis and visualization using MS Excel*

---

## 📌 Project Objective  
This is my **first data analysis project**, created to practice Excel dashboard design and visualization.  
The goal was to analyze **McDonald’s sales performance in South America (2022)** — focusing on key metrics such as total sales, profit, number of customers, and satisfaction levels.

---

## 🧾 Dataset Used  
- Dataset: McDonald’s South America sales data (practice dataset).  
- Data was cleaned, structured, and analyzed using Microsoft Excel.  

---

## 📊 Key Questions (KPIs)
- What were the **total sales, profit, and number of customers** in 2022?  
- How did sales trends change between **2021 and 2022**?  
- Which **countries** contributed the most to overall sales?  
- What are the main **customer satisfaction indicators**?  
- What percentage of business and satisfaction targets were achieved?

---

## ⚙️ Process
1. Verified and cleaned data for duplicates and missing values.  
2. Used **Pivot Tables** to summarize metrics like sales, profit, and customers.  
3. Created **charts** for sales trends, satisfaction levels, and regional sales.  
4. Designed a clear and simple **interactive dashboard** with slicers.  
5. Added map visualization to highlight sales by country.

## 💡 Project Insights  
- McDonald’s reached **$2.54B in sales** and **$890M in profit** in 2022.  
- Sales increased steadily during the second half of the year.  
- **Brazil and Argentina** were the top-performing countries.  
- Customer satisfaction was strong, especially in **availability (95%)** and **hygiene (93%)**.
